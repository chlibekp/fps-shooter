# Visual effects

All effects are generated by code (`Game/scripts/effects.gd`): textures are
gradients or images drawn at start-up, and there are no effect files.
Effects only show what the server reported (shots, impacts, explosions,
flashes, broken cells, gadget states); they never affect gameplay.

| Effect | How |
|--------|-----|
| Tracers | Thin emissive streaks that travel along the shot at about 600 m/s |
| Muzzle flash | A short additive particle burst, smoke puffs and a point light |
| Impacts | GPU particles per surface: dust and chips (concrete, brick, drywall, dirt), sparks with a light flash (metal, reinforced), shards (glass), splinters (wood), blood mist (players) |
| Bullet holes | Decals with a generated hole texture (scorched rim, dust ring), random rotation; the oldest disappear after 160 |
| Explosions | Fireball, rising smoke, dust, bouncing debris and sparks, a strong light and camera shake |
| Breaking cells | Chunks in the material's colour, plus dust (not for glass) |
| Flashbang | A very bright light, smoke, and a white-out on the HUD scaled by the server's blind intensity |
| Smoke grenade | A volumetric `FogVolume` ellipsoid with a 3D noise density texture. It grows to the gadget's radius over 2.5 s and fades when the grenade expires. Being real volumetric fog, it hides what is behind it like the server's smoke does. |

Particles use soft billboards, colour-ramp fades and proximity fade where
they meet geometry. Their collision is GPU particle collision; the map has no
particle colliders yet, so debris falls through the floor after its short
life rather than settling.

## Verified

* Impact dust and muzzle smoke are visible in the gameplay screenshots
  (software rendering).
* Explosions, flashbangs, smoke clouds and breaking have not been exercised
  in a rendered match yet.
